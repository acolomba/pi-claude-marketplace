<objective>
Make `/reload` move an installed plugin's MCP servers out of the legacy `<scopeRoot>/mcp.json` into `mcp-adapter.json`, in the final Phase 3/4 shape, and tell the user once.

Purpose: AMIG-01 and AMIG-03 at their thinnest end-to-end slice. Released builds (npm 0.19.x) wrote plugin servers into `mcp.json` under their declared names; this milestone writes them into `mcp-adapter.json` as `plugin_<plugin>_<server>_`, and nothing moves the old entries yet. This plan adds the reconcile step (D-05-16, D-05-17), its bridge helpers, its operations seam for the later write-order fault test, and the migration notice (D-05-12..15). It moves the plain case: an enabled record whose path source resolves offline as installable and declares every legacy name. Every other owner keeps its entries untouched here; plans 05-04 and 05-05 give those owners their rows.

Output: `bridges/mcp/legacy.ts`, `orchestrators/reconcile/mcp-migration.ts`, the apply wiring, `notifyMcpMigration`, two catalog blocks with their byte lock, paired unit tests at 100% direct coverage, the integration slice, one commit.
</objective>
