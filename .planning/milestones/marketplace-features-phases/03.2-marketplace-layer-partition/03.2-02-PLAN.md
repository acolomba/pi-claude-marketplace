<objective>
Merge marketplace entries field by field across a scope's two layers, local fields winning, so a
local declaration that sets no `autoupdate` follows the shared one and `noautoupdate --local` can be
a per-machine override (D-07). Plugin entries keep the wholesale merge (D-10). A real Pi proves the
inheritance through `marketplace list`, which reads the merged view. The comments that explained
the install's UAT-05 rule with the old wholesale merge are restated as present-tense facts.

Purpose: with LAYER-01 a marketplace can sit in both files; the merge decides what the scope means
then. Every merged-view reader (`list`, `info`, `plugin list`, `marketplace update`'s autoupdate
cascade) follows automatically.
Output: the field merge in `persistence/config-merge.ts` and its unit cases, the new
`features/marketplace/autoupdate.feature` with its first scenario, and the narrowed comments.
</objective>
