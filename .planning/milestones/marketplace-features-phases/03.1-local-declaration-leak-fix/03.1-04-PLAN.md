`claude-plugins.local.json` declares is refused before anything is staged or replaced, and its

SC4's refusal with a row that tells the user what to do. The refusal reads the same flag-hermetic
selector the write-back uses, so the decision before staging and the write after it cannot
disagree. Nameless forms keep covering every layer and are never refused (D-08).

refusals; the reinstall failed-row cause carrier; the reinstall S5 file naming; and their cases.
