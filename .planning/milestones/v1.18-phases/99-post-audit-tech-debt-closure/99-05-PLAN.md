<objective>
Export the manifest-membership discriminant and its successful-read derivation from a domain
module, and make all three absence-judging surfaces consume the exported value instead of each
re-implementing the rule (D-99-02a), backed by a drift gate so a fourth surface cannot copy the
idiom ungated.

Purpose: the milestone audit's integration checker called this the largest remaining warning and
stated the bar precisely: a fourth surface copying the lookup idiom without the read-success guard
would reintroduce the manifest-absence defect ungated. Meeting that bar means more than exporting
a type — the export removes the duplication that exists today, and the gate removes the duplication
that would exist tomorrow. Today the rule (exact string identity on the manifest's plugin names, no
case folding, no Unicode normalization) is written three times, and only one of those three writings
is guarded on a successful read.

Output: one domain module, three consumers, one gate, and not a single moved byte.
</objective>
