<objective>
Land the correctness spine of the phase in one plan: disable stops destroying the
installation record's inventory (ENBL-18 / D-100-10), and the two guards that
retention would otherwise break land with it — the hydrate suppression that keeps a
disabled plugin's hooks deregistered across `/reload` (ENBL-14 / D-100-05) and the
self-conflict exclusion that keeps `plugin enable` working (ENBL-19). Disable keeps
removing every artifact (ENBL-13 / D-100-04); only the record's *description* survives.

These three MUST NOT be separated by a wave boundary. Retention alone ships a build
where a disabled plugin's hooks re-register on the next reload and where enabling a
plugin that owns one skill throws `CrossPluginConflictError` against its own record.

Purpose: the installation record becomes a self-sufficient description of what was
installed, rather than a pointer into artifacts that must be inspected.
Output: a retained-inventory disable path with both guards, their tests, and the
de-duplicated `removePluginRecord` helper.
</objective>
