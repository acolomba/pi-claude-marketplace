## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| hook scripts to the machine | Hook scripts are real processes; a waiting script could outlive its scenario or the run. |
| hook recorder to the logs | The recorder writes what a hook received; it must not copy secrets from the environment. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-1-11 | Denial of service | waiting hook scripts in `hook-files.ts` | medium | mitigate | The release wait gives up after 60 seconds; every lifecycle hook writes a pid file, the profile Pi's release runs `assertStandInsStopped` over it, and Task 3 checks that no seed's hook script remains after the run. |
