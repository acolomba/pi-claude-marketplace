<objective>
Cap the blocking-lane timeout of a SessionEnd hook at Claude Code's 1.5 s
budget (D-BG-604): a plugin hook's own `timeout` does not raise the budget
upstream, so a longer declared value no longer holds a new session, a resume,
or a quit open. A declared value of 1.5 s or less is kept, an undeclared one
stays 1.5 s, and the background lane (`asyncRewake`) is unchanged. The unit
pair gains the cap cases, the comments of `timeout.ts` state the new rule, and
the SessionEnd clause of the `timeout` row in `docs/hooks-compatibility.md`
states it, with D-BG-605's open items.

Purpose: SEND-01 criterion 3 for a hook that declares a long timeout. The
behavioral proof is the `timeout: 60` rows of plan 06-03 (wave 2), which fail
without this cap; this plan proves the rule at the unit level first.
Output: the cap in `timeout.ts`, three new unit cases, one changed docs line,
and a SUMMARY.
