<objective>
Make `apply` converge on the catalog (APL-02, D-08, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-23, D-26). After its
plan, `apply` runs the same convergence planner `pending` uses over the scope as the plan
left it, materializes each revision move through a new runner entry,
`PluginUpdateOperations.convergePlugin`, and reports `(updated) <from> → <to>`, the
planner's holds and declines as warning rows, and a materialization failure as a
`(failed)` row. The runner gains a fourth policy knob, `searchReleaseTags`, which is
`false` for convergence, so `apply` takes the catalog version only (D-23).

Purpose: criterion 2 of the roadmap. A move goes through the shared runner `update` uses
(UPR-01), not reinstall, which would delete the plugin data directory and overwrite edited
agents (D-26). Growth-only moves join in plan 29-13, which also retires the backfill.

Output: the policy knob and the tag-free gate, the runner entry and its operation
binding, the convergence step and its rows, the catalog states, and the apply scenarios,
including a cold git clone with its request log.
</objective>
