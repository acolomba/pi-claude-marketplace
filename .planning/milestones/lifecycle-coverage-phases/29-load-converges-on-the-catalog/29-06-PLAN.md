<objective>
Preview marketplace adds and removes (D-20). A hand-edited config that declares a
marketplace now waits for `apply` to add it (a git source clones, and a load stays
offline), so `pending` lists it under a `(will add)` header. A recorded marketplace no
config declares lists under a `(will remove)` header with its `(will uninstall)`
children. Two new members join `MarketplaceStatus`, through every closed-set site
(D-15). This supersedes WILL-01 / WILL-03 ("add is immediate, not previewed").

Purpose: APL-01/APL-04 say `pending` lists every change `apply` makes. Plan 29-02 already
moved the add and the remove into `apply`; this plan makes `pending` show them. Plan 29-08
then shows the same rows at load.

Output: the vocabulary, the pending projection, the closed-set gates, the catalog and the
references, and pending and apply scenarios for both actions.
</objective>
