Preview catalog moves (APL-02, D-10, D-14, D-15). A new network-free planner,
`orchestrators/reconcile/convergence.ts`, finds every recorded plugin whose catalog
candidate on disk differs from its record, and `pending`'s composer renders each as a new

plan 29-11 adds git sources and the supported-set growth test, plan 29-10 the blocked
moves.

analog, because the plan has no update bucket") is superseded.

owner test, the pending rows, the scenarios whose load now previews a move, and the
catalog and references.
