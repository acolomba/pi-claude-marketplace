reason set with `dependency installed` on every pin that counts it, and lands
the projection arm and catalog state for the row a reload will render.

plan 09-03 can wire the apply step and plan 09-04 can document the failure form
without touching the vocabulary again. The convergence proof (D-09-16) lands
here because the bucket is what it exercises.

61st reason with its projection arm, the `reconcile-dependency-installed`
catalog state, and the convergence cases.
