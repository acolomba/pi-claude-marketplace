<objective>
Remove the second live `__deps` bag — the one on `ReinstallPluginOptions` and
`ReinstallPluginsOptions` — by promoting its three members to typed top-level options fields in the
form three sibling orchestrators already use.

Purpose: `D-05-01` forbids a `__deps` member outright, and `D-07-18` as amended names this bag
alongside `__operations` precisely because removing one and gating the other is the split that lets
a class survive. A `__`-prefix gate that shipped with this bag in place would land red against its
own tree, and `07-13-PLAN.md` cannot ship until it is gone.

Output: `stateTransaction?`, `removeDataDir?`, and `cloneCacheSeam?` as named members of the two
options interfaces, `ReinstallPluginDeps` retired, and all 54 test call sites migrated with no
assertion traded away.
</objective>
