<objective>
Find and close the composed-path gap that criterion 4 names: a gate that addresses its targets by
a path assembled from segments is invisible to a literal-match stale-path scan, so it can go on
reporting success over targets that stopped resolving.

Purpose: this is not hypothetical. `tests/architecture/hooks-lifecycle.test.ts` and
`tests/architecture/import-boundaries.test.ts` both kept addressing retired ledger modules through
`path.join`, and the specific stale names were repaired without anything being put in place to stop
it recurring. A gate whose target list silently stops resolving is worse than a missing gate,
because it buys confidence it has not earned.

Output: a measured census of every composed production target under `tests/architecture/**`, those
targets moved into the registry, and each migrated gate proving both that its targets resolve and
that it inspected a non-zero number of them.
</objective>
