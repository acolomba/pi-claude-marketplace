<objective>
Decouple the dispatch key domain from the bucket-A admission tuple (D-87-04) and
re-point every test/fixture that uses `Stop` as its canonical "unsupported /
non-bucket-A event" example to `Notification` (D-87-06) — all while
`BUCKET_A_EVENTS` stays at 8, so this plan changes NO admission behavior and the
full suite stays green. This is the enabling ground for Plan 02's admission
cutover: once the three total dispatch tables key on a `DispatchableEvent`
subset (still the current 8), growing the admission tuple to 10 in Plan 02 no
longer forces Stop/StopFailure translators (which are Phase 88 work).

Purpose: Growing `BUCKET_A_EVENTS` 8→10 would otherwise break the typecheck at
six `Record<BucketAEvent>` totality sites AND flip a large set of tests that
rely on `Stop` being unsupported. Landing the decouple and the example-event
rename first — while types are still identical and behavior is unchanged — keeps
every commit green and shrinks Plan 02 to the genuine admission change.

Output: `DISPATCHABLE_EVENTS` / `DispatchableEvent` subset in hook-events.ts;
three production dispatch tables + three translator-test tables re-keyed to the
subset; `Notification` as the new canonical unsupported example across the suite
and the two renamed synthetic fixtures.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
Detector: pluralization. D-87-04 splits what was ONE key domain (`BucketAEvent`,
serving both admission AND the dispatch/rewake tables) into TWO.

- **Noun now primary:** the admitted bucket-A event union `BucketAEvent`
  (grows to 10 in Plan 02) — admission is the general representation.
- **Decision:** promote. `DispatchableEvent` (the 8-event dispatchable subset)
  is the demoted variant detail keyed to the dispatch/rewake/translator tables;
  Phase 88 folds it back up to full parity when the two translators land.
- **Rationale:** admission is the broader closed set; dispatch is a strict
  subset that trails it by exactly the events whose translators are not yet
  wired.
- **Invariant:** the subset tuple is pinned `as const satisfies readonly
  BucketAEvent[]`, making "every dispatchable event is an admitted event" a
  compile-time invariant — mirrors the existing `TOOL_EVENTS` subset pattern at
  `hook-events.ts:69-78`.
- **Reversibility:** reversible (D-87-04) — Phase 88 removes the subset.
</assumption_delta_decision>
