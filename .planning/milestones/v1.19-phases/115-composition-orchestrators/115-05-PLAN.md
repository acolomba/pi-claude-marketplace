<objective>
Prove the load-time reconcile cascade exhaustively and make its producer contract compile-time.

Purpose: `apply.ts` is the largest gap in the phase — 31 branches, one function, and 159 lines
short — the only pair with a genuine reachability wall, and the only one with cross-module
contamination. Three of its fifteen outcome kinds have never been produced by any case, one of
its four source-mismatch causes is proved, and eight of its thirty-three cases do not test
`apply.ts` at all: they read production source text for seven other modules and assert regular
expressions against their comments.

The reachability wall is settled, not open. Three guards in the cascade check whether a
collaborator returned no outcome in orchestrated mode. Reading each producer shows that the
undefined return is the standalone-mode arm — the orchestrated arm always returns a typed
outcome — so those branches cannot be reached through the real collaborators, and D-115-03
forbids reaching them by injection. D-115-10 resolves it the way the repository already
resolved it once for the toggle loop: give the three producers the same mode-discriminated
overload the enable-and-disable entrypoint carries, then delete the guards, so a dropped row
becomes a compile error instead of a silent continue. That is an operator-approved exception to
the rule that supporting edits stay inside one pair, and it is type-level only, so the three
Phase-114 owner suites are expected to pass unmodified. Confirm that expectation; do not assume
it, and do not edit a completed pair silently.

Sizing note. Research put the honest matrix at 66 cells, or about 54 once the continuation
cells fold into the failure cells, against 33 existing cases of which 8 are deleted outright —
roughly 40 to 45 net-new cases plus 25 rewrites, landing near 3,000 to 3,500 lines. That is
larger than one comfortable executor pass, so this plan is split into three ordered tasks
within the single pair rather than into a ninth plan: the pair contract permits exactly one
production pair per plan and per commit, and the roadmap fixes the phase at eight plans. Do not
shrink the matrix to a representative sample; if a task will not fit, stop after it and report
progress.

Output: a compile-time producer contract, three fewer impossible branches, no source-text
pins, and an exhaustive cascade owner at 100 percent direct coverage.
