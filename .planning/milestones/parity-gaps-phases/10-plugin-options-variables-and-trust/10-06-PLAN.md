## Planner decisions

- **Token: `options not set`.** It follows the row grammar `<name> (status) {reason}` and
  upstream's own wording for the state ("userConfig options not yet set"). It appends at the
  tail of the catalog order.
- **The keys ride the note and `info`, never the token.** The `info` line names the keys;
  its wording is the executor's, in the voice of the lines `composeResolvedComponents` builds
  today, and it names no value.
- **An earlier release's partial record shows the same reason.** Its kind list holds
  `userConfig`, and its options are in fact not delivered until a restage (D-PG10-15).
