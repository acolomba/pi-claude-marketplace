<objective>
Send the session-event fields that Claude Code's hooks receive: `custom_instructions` on `PreCompact`, `model` and `session_title` on `SessionStart`, and `session_title` on `UserPromptSubmit`.

Purpose: a `PreCompact` hook can keep what the user asked the compaction to keep, and a `SessionStart` hook can act on the model and the session's name. The bridge sends none of the three today, though Pi has each value.
Output: the field in the three translators, the two values on the translation context, two harness steps (a compaction with instructions and a named session), the new scenarios, and the new columns in the tables that hold a `PreCompact` or a `SessionStart` row.
</objective>
