<objective>
Dispatch the admitted `UserPromptExpansion` event: serve it from the composite handler, with its filter, notices, and context, and register the `input` handler that runs it for a line an injected resolver names.

Purpose: plan 06-08 admitted the event and routed its hooks, and nothing dispatches them. Pi raises its `input` event before it expands a skill or a template, which is where Claude Code runs the event. A block must stop the expansion before any later handler rewrites the line.
Output: a buffered prompt context that carries its event, the event's arm in every switch of the dispatch owner, the block text of either prompt event, a second `input` registration that takes the typed-line resolver as an injected option, and the gates that pin 14 registrations. The resolver and the dispatch scenarios land in plan 06-12; until then no line dispatches the event.
</objective>
