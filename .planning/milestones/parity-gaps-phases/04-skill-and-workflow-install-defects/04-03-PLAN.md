<objective>
Make a relative link from a staged skill to a sibling skill of the same plugin resolve to the folder the sibling is staged in.

The local official marketplace copy holds 45 such links in three plugins.
Output: extensions/pi-claude-marketplace/domain/skill-sibling-paths.ts with its owner test, the wiring in extensions/pi-claude-marketplace/bridges/skills/stage.ts, and features/skill/sibling-links.feature.
