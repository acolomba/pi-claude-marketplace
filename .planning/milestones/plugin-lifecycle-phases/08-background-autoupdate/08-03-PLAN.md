<objective>
Add the one passive notice of AUP-03: the load-origin raw kind
`background-update`, through every exhaustive notification consumer, its
emitter `notifyBackgroundUpdate`, and the pass's single call to it after the
scope loop; then document and byte-lock it (D-PL8-10, D-PL8-11, D-PL8-15,
D-PL8-16).

Purpose: a background update that moved at least one plugin tells the user
which plugins moved and that `/reload` applies them, in Claude Code's wording
with Pi's reload command; a run that changed nothing stays silent.

Output: the new kind in `shared/notification-{types,resolve,grammar,json,wire,dispatch}.ts`,
the regenerated `schema/claude-plugin.schema.json`, the pass's collection and
notice, the owner and architecture test updates, a `docs/json-output.md`
kind section, and a `docs/output-catalog.md` block locked by a new architecture test.

Why one atomic first task: the new `LoadSurface` member makes every
exhaustive consumer and every mapped-type test table fail to typecheck until
it has an arm; the wire-schema, json-output-docs, and catalog-wire gates fail
until the schema, the doc section, and the sample land; and `fallow dead-code`
fails on `notifyBackgroundUpdate` until its production caller (the pass)
lands. The commit hook runs all of them, so they share one commit.
</objective>
