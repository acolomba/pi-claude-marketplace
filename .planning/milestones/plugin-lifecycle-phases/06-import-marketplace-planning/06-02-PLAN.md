<objective>
Make every orchestrated failure outcome that MIM-03's rows read carry the rollback partials the failure left: the install outcome of a failed dependency (D-PL6-04), the enable outcome (D-PL6-11), and the reconcile failed-install outcome type (D-PL6-05).

Purpose: import and apply cannot show install's per-phase detail from a boolean or from nothing. Today `handleCascadeThrow` drops a failed dependency's partials from the orchestrated outcome, `outcomeToTypedResult` drops an enable's partials, and `PluginInstallFailedOutcome` has no field for them and pins `cause` to two error classes. These are small additive edits to files other wave-1 phases own (Phase 4 owns other functions of `install-flow.ts`; Phase 1 owns `enable-disable.ts` and `apply-outcomes.ts`; Phase 2 owns `enable-disable.ts`'s enable warning surface), so they land first, alone, and touch only the named functions and types.

Output: three producer-side contract changes, each with its owner test.

Spec-less probe fallback: disabled for this run. Edge cases come from 06-CONTEXT.md and 06-RESEARCH.md (Patterns 5, 6, 8 and Pitfalls 5, 6).
</objective>
