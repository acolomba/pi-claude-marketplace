<objective>
Close DFEN-01 at the schema boundary, and prove the phase's no-observable-change
promise where it is easiest to break: the install path.

The schema half is declarative and its blast radius was chosen deliberately. One
malformed `defaultEnabled` rejects the entire `marketplace.json`, exactly as a
non-string `version` does today — and exactly unlike the per-plugin containment the
same test file already records for a broken source reference. Those two precedents
sit a hundred lines apart and look contradictory to a reader who does not know
which one applies, so the new test states which it is and why.

The characterization half is the narrow proof that the schema and resolver edits
alone changed nothing. A plugin declaring `defaultEnabled: false` must still install
enabled at this phase, with its artifacts materialized. Acting on the value is the
next phase's work, and the fastest way to discover that it leaked early is to assert
today's behavior now rather than after it has shifted.

Purpose: pin the two failure surfaces DFEN-01 names, and pin the boundary this phase
must not cross.
Output: the accept/reject matrix for both compiled validators, the whole-manifest
rejection proof with its contrast stated, two fixture knobs, and three
characterization tests covering install and the one read surface most likely to
notice a new entry field.
</objective>
