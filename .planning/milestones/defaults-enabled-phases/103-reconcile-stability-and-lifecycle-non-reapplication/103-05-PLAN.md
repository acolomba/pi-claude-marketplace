<objective>
Close the phase's two bookkeeping items and take the boundary gate.

The first is a decision that is currently held only in a plan document. D-103-01
settles a genuinely balanced product question — should `install` honor a config
entry that already says `enabled: false` — and settles it against symmetry,
because DFEN-08 requires that a manifest declaring the field true, and a
manifest that never mentions it, behave byte-identically to pre-milestone across
six surfaces. There is no form of the widening that survives that requirement.

and the convergence half that makes the behavior defensible is asserted nowhere.

The second is the roadmap gloss the Phase 102 verification flagged and overrode.
Its illustrative clause describes an outcome the shipped install verb does not
produce, and leaving it standing means a later reader takes it for a promise.

Purpose: an unexplained pin invites a well-meaning symmetry fix, and a
misdescribing criterion invites a build against it. Both are cheap now and
expensive after the milestone ships.
Output: one amended matrix row with its rationale and its convergence
assertion, one reworded roadmap clause, and the phase-boundary `npm run check`.
</objective>
