<objective>
Make `update` treat workflows as a sixth bridge, and make the three already-widened failure slots
reachable.

Purpose: an update today reports success while the envelopes on disk still hold the previous version's
executable script. A workflow the new version added never appears, one it removed stays installed and
runnable, and no row, reason token or warning marks any of it. Three closed sets already carry a
`workflows` member that this verb cannot produce, so the compile-forcing signal those sets exist to give
is already spent for this axis — the only thing that makes them honest is a verb that can reach them.

Output: four new seams inside the update ledger, a record policy that is deliberately unlike its five
siblings, and one case per widened slot driven through the verb.
</objective>

arms whose commit returns an optional cleanup leak — a non-`undefined` return is pushed as its own typed
