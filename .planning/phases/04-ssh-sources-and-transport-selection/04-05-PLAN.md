<objective>
Finish the marketplace transport of `marketplace add` (SG-SSH-02, SG-SSH-03; 04-EVIDENCE
"Progress lines and errors"): when the first transport fails, retry with the other one, and
when both fail, report one error with both attempts (upstream `wqo`). Then the harness work:
the ssh stand-in refuses the transport of a denied or host-key host as its probe does, it
gains an arm that authenticates but finds no repository, the scenario variables reach a
scenario's own Pi, and the Device Flow scenarios state the user's ssh.

The retry and the Device Flow re-baseline land together on purpose: a refused sign-in fails
the HTTPS attempt, and the SSH retry then changes those scenarios' failure tables, so
splitting them would leave a wave gate red. 04-04 kept the probe and the attempt order.

Purpose: a user whose first transport fails still adds the marketplace over the other one,
and a user who sees both fail gets one error naming both.
Output: the plan's `after`, the runner's retry and both-failed paths, the add's
`beforeRetry`, the stand-in refusals and the arm without repository access, the scenario
variables with their hermetic test, the fallback, PREFER_HTTPS, and CLAUDE_CODE_REMOTE
scenarios, the re-baselined Device Flow scenarios, and auth-e2e on the stand-in.
</objective>
