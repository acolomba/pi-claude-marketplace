<objective>
Measure the final tree once, generate the committed pin from that measurement, and bring every
record that carries a coverage claim into agreement with it.

`D-08-08` orders two full report runs, and this is the second: after the rewrites, the port, and
every test written for a reachable arm. The pin is generated from THIS run, so the committed
artifact reflects the final tree rather than an intermediate one. Two full sweeps is roughly
sixteen minutes of wall clock, and that is the price of a baseline that is measured rather than
inherited.

`D-08-04a` requires a coordinated rewrite. Four artifacts carry the same stale claims —
`.planning/ROADMAP.md` §"Phase 8", `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` (`RCOV-01` and `RCOV-02`),
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `scripts/revalidation.mjs` — and they are rewritten in one change, in the
form `RVAL-04` requires. A fifth file is drawn in by the mechanism rather than by the decision:
`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`'s clause text is sha256-sealed in both `scripts/revalidation.mjs` and
`01-REVALIDATION.json`, and `node scripts/revalidation.mjs scope-impact --check` refuses unless all
three agree.

The specific trap, recorded so nobody walks into it: the roadmap said seven shortfalls and the
first measured answer was also seven — a DIFFERENT seven. A count that happens to match is the most
dangerous kind of stale claim, because it survives a sanity check. `RCOV-02`'s rewrite therefore
names the modules and their readings and never restates a number.

Purpose: make every coverage claim in the repository equal to a measurement taken from the final
tree.
Output: a regenerated pin, an `--all` arm that exits 0, and four records plus one sealed signature
set that agree with it.
</objective>
