<objective>
Make `/claude:plugin info` list what a root-source entry installs: the listed skills, each as one skill.

The lenient listing for a plugin the resolver refuses must follow the same rule.
Output: extensions/pi-claude-marketplace/orchestrators/plugin/info.ts with its owner cases, and two scenarios in features/skill/root-source-entries.feature.
</objective>
