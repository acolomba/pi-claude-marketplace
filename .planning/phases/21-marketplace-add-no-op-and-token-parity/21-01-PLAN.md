<objective>
Make a same-source `marketplace add` / `bootstrap` a `(skipped) {already
added}` no-op at info, name marketplace-manifest faults on `add`/`update` with
`narrowProbeError`'s tokens, and reject a flag after `marketplace help` as
`Unknown flag: "<flag>".` (21-CONTEXT.md D-01..D-09).
</objective>
