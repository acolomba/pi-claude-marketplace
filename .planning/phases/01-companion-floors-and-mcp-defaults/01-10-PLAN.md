What Claude Code does with a tool call's time, from `https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp`
as the research fetched it on 2026-11-11 (Claude Code 2.1.322 is the installed version):

- The per-server `timeout` is a hard wall-clock limit per tool call; progress notifications
  do not extend it. A value below 1000 is ignored and falls through to `MCP_TOOL_TIMEOUT`, or
  to its default of about 28 hours.
- A tool call that sends no response and no progress notification for the idle window aborts
  with an error. The idle window defaults to 5 minutes for HTTP, SSE, WebSocket, and
  claude.ai connector servers, and to 30 minutes for stdio servers.
- A per-server `timeout` of at least 1000 also acts as a floor on the idle timeout.
- Remote servers also have a second timer, per request, up to the server's first response
  byte.
