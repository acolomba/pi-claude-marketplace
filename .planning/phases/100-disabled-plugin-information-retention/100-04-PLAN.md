<objective>
Stop treating a disabled plugin as a foreign shape on the `info` surface. Today the
disabled scopes are diverted out of the found set before the block builder runs, so
they get a bare row and force a second notification for mixed results. Route them
through the same builder every other installed record uses (D-100-08): a disabled
plugin that is still declared resolves from the manifest exactly as an uninstalled one
does, and a disabled plugin the manifest dropped resolves from its own record — which
is precisely the population the retained inventory exists for.

Two things must be carried deliberately through the reroute rather than inherited: the
fetch-skip note must survive, and the disabled status must be injected ahead of the
persisted-status derivation or a disabled plugin renders as installed.

Purpose: `info` stops losing information about a plugin the user disabled.
Output: the widened row status subset, the collapsed partition, and a single
producer-reported skip-reason carrier.
</objective>

## Decisions closed by this plan

The research pass left two questions open and asked the planner to settle them rather
than leave them to the executor. Both are settled here, with rationale.

- **Disabled plus manifest-absent: one skip row or two, and which reason?** ONE row,
  reporting the already-disabled cause. Rationale: the fetch-skip note answers "why did
  the fetch do nothing", and disabled-ness is the proximate answer — a disabled scope is
  skipped whatever the manifest says, while manifest absence only skips a scope that was
  otherwise fetchable. The inventory row keeps the manifest-absence reason because it
  answers a different question, "what constrains the user next" (`D-100-07`). The
  mechanism that makes one row structural rather than test-enforced is the single
  optional field: two lists could concatenate, one field cannot. The consequence for the
  catalog's mixed skip state — whose prose assumes the two causes are scope-disjoint —
  is a prose amendment, scheduled in the following plan.

- **Is the old disabled-scope block builder deleted or repurposed?** DELETED, along with
  its scope interface. Rationale: keeping a thin scope list purely to feed the skip
  emitter is the smaller diff but leaves two carriers for one fact; deleting it is what
  makes the mixed-message-kind comment removable rather than half-true, and it relieves
  the notify control flow's cognitive-complexity budget, which the partition helper was
  extracted to protect in the first place.
