<objective>
Close the gate's second stage and report every remaining arm.

Stage one answers only what a tag listing can answer. Stage two is what catches
a repository with no release tags at all and a path source whose current copy
has drifted out of range: the candidate resolves exactly as it does today, and
the version that comes out is re-checked against the same intersected range
before anything is written. In range the update proceeds -- and when it got there
by falling back to a marketplace's current copy, the success row says so with
the token that already exists for that fact. Out of range it is held, naming
only the dependents that actually reject what was fetched.

The same plan makes the already-at-the-ceiling case legible (`{up-to-date}` plus
a cause line disclosing the range and its holders) and gives the autoupdate
cascade the same cause line and the same warning severity the manual cascade
renders.

Purpose: the arms UPDT-01 and UPDT-02 are unmet for without stage two, plus
D-10-12's deliberate severity divergence stated where a future reader will find
it.

Output: `admitResolvedVersion`, the one disclosure carrier, the
`{dependency current copy}` axis, the up-to-date disclosure, the autoupdate
cascade's cause line, and four catalog states.
</objective>
