Tarball recipe (verified with npm 11.20 during research): a gzip (node:zlib gzipSync) of a ustar archive with two
512-byte-aligned entries, `package/package.json` = {"name":"<name>","version":"<version>","main":"index.js"} and
`package/index.js` = module.exports = "<name> <version>"; then two 512-byte zero blocks. Each 512-byte header:
name at offset 0 (utf8, 100 bytes), "0000644\0" at 100, "0000000\0" at 108 and 116, the size as 11 octal digits plus
"\0" at 124, "00000000000\0" at 136, eight spaces at 148 while summing, "0" at 156, "ustar\0" at 257, "00" at 263;
then write the byte sum as 6 octal digits plus "\0 " at 148. integrity = "sha512-" + base64 sha512 of the gzip
bytes. Served at GET /<name>/-/<name>-<version>.tgz; every other path answers 404 and is logged.
