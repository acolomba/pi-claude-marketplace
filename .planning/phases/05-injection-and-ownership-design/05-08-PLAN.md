<objective>
Route plugin install, update, reinstall, and uninstall invalidation through the same composition-owned completion-cache instance while retaining transaction cohesion and future split-compatible contracts (D-05-05, D-05-06, D-05-09).

Purpose: Make every successful plugin mutation invalidate the cache that subsequent completion reads actually use, without optional cache-drop selection or transaction drift.
Output: Required cache propagation across plugin handlers and orchestrators, public mutation-to-completion regressions, and removal of the reinstall cache-selection seam without any Phase 6 module split.
</objective>

## Edge assumptions

- Plans 05-05 and 05-06 complete first. Their summaries govern the final runtime, transaction-port, cache-contract, and transition-instance names.
- Before removing a direct invalidator import, type alias, or option member, the executor runs a current production-consumer census. A newly discovered genuine consumer must migrate to the owned cache contract with unchanged public behavior.
- Exactly one cache lifetime exists across registered completion reads and plugin mutations during this wave. A handler, orchestrator, cascade callback, reconcile child, or per-target loop must not construct another cache.
- Plan 05-09 replaces the bounded transition ownership with extension-lifecycle construction. This plan must leave a required, production-used contract that 05-09 can rebind without changing plugin business logic.
- Invalidation remains post-commit hygiene. It cannot enter a prepare, intent-mark, physical-replace, state-finalize, or rollback phase, and an early failure cannot evict rows as if a mutation committed.
- A cache-drop failure retains each command's present warning or silent-hygiene contract and cannot prevent later data-dir or clone cleanup that currently runs.
- applyReconcile and bootstrapClaudePlugin remain the two approved composition exceptions. Cache propagation cannot replace their real children or reduce their complete observable assertions.
- Phase 6 owns global-patch removal and large source/test ownership splits. Stage, PID, info, and uninstall decomposition stay outside this plan.
