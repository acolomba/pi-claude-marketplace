## Design (D-PL2-18)

**One step per key.** `dependenciesFirst` (convergence.ts:665) builds a `Map` keyed by the step key,
so two steps that share a key collapse into one. A waiting record's move and its enable
therefore stay one `CascadeStep`, which carries a new `waiting: true` marker. Each consumer turns
it into two rows. The move is always planned, from the cascade, whether or not the enable comes.

**apply still runs the move and the enable back to back in one `runStep`.** A deferred record's
move only refreshes its pin (it answers `{already disabled}`); the enable then materializes the
new version. Running them together keeps WR-C: if the enable fails, the pin goes back and the
move reports one `(failed)` row.

**Row contract** (`<dep>` is the missing dependency; `<W>` is the waiting record; versions here
are illustrative):

| Shape | `pending` lists (before `apply`) | `apply` reports |
|-------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| The install, the move and the enable land | `<dep> (will install)`, `<W> 1.0.0 → 2.0.0 (will update)`, `<W> (will enable)` | `<dep> (installed) {dependency installed}`, `<W> 1.0.0 → 2.0.0 (updated)`, `<W> 2.0.0 (installed)` (an enable renders as `installed`) |
| The install fails or is refused | the same three rows: `pending` cannot foresee a refusal | `<dep> (failed) {…}` with its cause (D-PL2-11), then `<W> 1.0.0 (skipped) {dependency failed}`. No row for the enable that did not run (WR-B). `<W>`'s record is untouched. |
| The install lands, then the move or the enable fails | the same three rows | `<W>`'s one `(failed)` row; a failed enable restores the pin (WR-C) |
| A move that declares `<W>` (WR-06) | its `will update` row, after `<W>`'s rows | `(updated)` on success. When `<W>`'s enable did not come: `(skipped) {dependency failed}`, or the judgement's `(disabled)` row in its place (WR-08). |
| A deferred record in the cascade (WR-02) | one `will update` row | one net `(updated)` row (unchanged) |

**Consequences to pin, and to state in the SUMMARY:**
- A growth-only move (D-10) of a waiting record follows the same rule. Its move row is the same
  row `pending` lists (`enabledMoveOutcome`: `(updated)` with equal versions and
  `{components now supported}`), followed by its enable row.
- The blocked waiting step joins `failed.all`, as every blocked step does in `runSteps`. So a
  dependent that the judgement then disables for that record takes the cascade's row:
  `{dependency failed}` with "<W> did not update; run apply again once it can, or uninstall
  <dependent>" (`cascadeDisabledOutcome`). The LOAD-01 "Enable <W> or uninstall <dependent>"
  remedy no longer applies there, and that remedy would be refused anyway, because `<W>`'s
  dependency is missing.
- When the read after the install fails, the fresh plan has no enables. Every waiting move then
  reports `(skipped) {dependency failed}` beside the `state.json` row, as its dependents already do
  in that path.
