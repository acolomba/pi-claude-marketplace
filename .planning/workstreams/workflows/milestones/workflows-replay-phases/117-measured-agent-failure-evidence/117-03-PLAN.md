<objective>
Make the records agree with what happened, and take the phase through its gate.

Purpose: an archived verification record asserts, in four places and in its most
persuasive prose, that a live canary ran and closed. Its own reason-for-human field says
that canary was never exercised, and the driver it names does not exist on this branch at
all. A record that contradicts itself is worse than one that admits a gap: the confident
half is what a later reader believes, and it is the half that is false.

Two live pointers name that same never-landed driver as the standing live seam, and four
of this phase's own success criteria were written against premises the research overturned
-- a filename, an engine version, a census figure, and an ambiguous reading of which side
of the contradiction wins. A phase measured against criteria it cannot satisfy fails for
the wrong reason.

Output: a surgically corrected archived record that keeps the evidence of its own
over-claim, two seam pointers aimed at a file that exists, criteria the phase can be
honestly measured against, a green project gate, and one place where everything this phase
leaves open is written down.
</objective>
