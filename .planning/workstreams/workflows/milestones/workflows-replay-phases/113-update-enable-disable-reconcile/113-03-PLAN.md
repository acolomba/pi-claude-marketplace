<objective>
Put the staged workflow names on the projection both enable verbs read, and pin the load-time
no-re-materialization guarantee with a case.

Purpose: enabling a plugin re-materializes its envelopes and disabling it takes them away, but nothing
outside the ledger can currently learn WHICH envelopes a materialization placed. That set difference —
recorded names minus newly staged names — is the fact the next plan's reload remedy is computed from, and
it exists nowhere else. Separately, the claim that a reload does not rewrite envelopes on every load is
true today for two structural reasons and is proved by nothing; a phase that adds a sixth materialized
kind is the wrong time to leave it that way.

Output: one required member on the ledger's outward projection with its producer and its reader, and a
double-reconcile idempotence case with a negative control.
</objective>
