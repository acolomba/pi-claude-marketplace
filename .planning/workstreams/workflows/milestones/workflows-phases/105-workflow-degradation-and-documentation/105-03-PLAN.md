<objective>
Make "the artifacts do not depend on the probe" a machine-checked property rather than a claim,
at three strengths: a byte-equality pair over two probe states, a boundary gate that forbids
the bridge from reading the probe at all, and a live-canary assertion against a real engine.

Purpose: WDEP-03 is what makes the write-anyway choice correct rather than merely harmless. The
regression it guards against is specific and plausible — someone reads "degrade when the engine
is absent" as "skip staging when the engine is absent", the install then succeeds with a marker
and writes nothing, and installing the engine later finds nothing to register.

Output: the probe-independence pair with its non-vacuity clause, a new architectural boundary
gate, and a fourth assertion on the existing live canary.
</objective>
