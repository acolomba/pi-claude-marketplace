<structural_decision>
The union listing is **DELETED, not corrected.** Three reasons, in order of
weight:

1. **The section it sits in opens by promising not to do this.** Line 15: "This
   section points at those definitions; it does not duplicate them. Read the
   source." The listing is a verbatim duplicate of a nineteen-arm TypeScript
   union, inside the section that promises to point rather than duplicate. Its
   own one-line preamble (line 33) then tells the reader to read the source for
   the canonical membership — and the listing immediately re-enumerates it.

2. **It is the second copy, and it is the copy that rotted.** Everything the
   listing carries beyond the arm names is per-arm field discipline, which the
   same section already states grouped by field at lines 66-71. The by-field
   block stayed closer to true (it already says 19); the listing drifted by
   three missing members plus one retired interface name. Two statements of one
   fact, one of them unbound, is the defect — not the numbers.

3. **Glyphs and rendered bytes are not this document's job.** Line 11 assigns
   them to `docs/output-catalog.md`, and the byte-equality gate at
   `tests/architecture/catalog-uat.test.ts` already owns them. The glyph
   annotations in the listing were a third copy of a contract two other
   artifacts hold.

What replaces it: a one-sentence source pointer, plus a nineteen-row `status x
field` table that states the per-arm discipline **once**, in a shape a test can
parse. The table is not a smaller re-enumeration — it is the by-field prose of
lines 66-71 turned into the same information in a checkable layout, and the
prose rationale (decision IDs, carve-out reasoning) stays beneath it.

**What is deliberately NOT touched:** `docs/adr/v2-001-structured-notify.md:101`
carries the same stale listing. It stays. This repository already states the
principle, at `tests/architecture/no-stale-test-citations.test.ts` lines 18-25,
which excludes `docs/adr/` "because these are dated records rather than
descriptions of today's tree — a citation in them is correct history, and
'fixing' it would falsify the record." The ADR records what was decided across
the v2 migration; the guide states what is true now. Only the guide is corrected,
and the new pin is scoped to the guide alone for the same reason.
</structural_decision>
