<objective>
Move the plugin clone seam onto the derived wire URL: `resolvePluginPin` derives it from the source it
already holds, and the two materialize functions stop deriving anything at all.

Purpose: three of the four seam sites live in `orchestrators/plugin/clone-cache.ts`. Two of them hold
only a `cloneUrl` string, and the `url` arm of the derivation needs `source.raw` — unrecoverable from
that string once the parse-time strip has run. So the wire URL is computed by the nine callers that
already hold the parsed source and threaded in as a required `networkUrl` field. Required, not
optional: a defaulted field would compile clean at every un-updated site, which is the silent-omission
class this repo has already shipped three times in one milestone.

Output: `networkUrl` on both materialize arg types, `resolvePluginPin` on the new derivation, nine
threaded call sites, and the direct unit suite rewritten from "the seam appends a suffix" to "the seam
forwards what it was handed and keys the disk off the other value".

**Task 1 touches six production files. That is deliberate and is a context-cost judgement, not an
oversight:** five of the six edits are a single added object-literal field at a line named below, and
the required field means no smaller split has a verifiable intermediate state — a task that landed the
seam without its callers would leave the compiler red with nothing runnable to check.

**Expected mid-phase red:** after Task 1 the plugin flow, bootstrap, reconcile, register, integration
and `marketplace update` suites go red, because their remote allowlists still admit only the suffixed
form. That is discovery, not regression — plan 03 owns the audit that closes them. Do NOT restore a
suffix anywhere to make one of them pass.
</objective>
